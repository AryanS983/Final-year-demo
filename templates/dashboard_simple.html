📚 Understanding Your Risk Metrics

Industry Standard Risk Ratios

📈 Sharpe Ratio

Measures risk-adjusted return. Higher values indicate better performance per unit of risk. Above 1.0 is good, above 2.0 is excellent.

📉 Sortino Ratio

Similar to Sharpe but only considers downside volatility. Better for evaluating funds with asymmetric risk profiles.

⚖️ Beta

Measures sensitivity to market movements. Beta > 1 means more volatile than market, < 1 means less volatile.

🎯 Alpha

Measures excess return compared to benchmark, adjusted for market risk. Positive alpha indicates outperformance.

📊 Information Ratio

Measures active return per unit of tracking error. Higher values indicate better active management.

🏔️ Calmar Ratio

Annual return divided by maximum drawdown. Higher values indicate better risk-adjusted performance.

Key Performance Metrics

📈 Total Return

The overall percentage gain or loss during your selected period. This shows how much your investment would have grown.

⚡ Daily Volatility

Average daily price fluctuation. Higher values mean more unpredictable daily movements.

📊 Annual Volatility

Yearly price fluctuation measure. Above 18% is high risk, below 12% is low risk.

✅ Positive Days %

Percentage of days when the fund value increased. Above 55% indicates good performance.

💰 Best/Worst Day

Single best and worst daily performance. Shows the range of daily price movements.

📅 Trading Days

Total number of trading days in your selected period. Used for calculating all metrics.
